# PrismPack app icon: final layers for Icon Composer

Chosen 29 Oct 2026: the rainbow deck with violet in front and the 3D prism.
Generated by `../prismpack-icon-concepts/generate_final.py`.

Every SVG is one solid shape at full opacity on a 1024 x 1024 canvas, with no
baked-in edges, glow or glint. Icon Composer adds the glass, translucency
and highlights. `preview.png` only roughly approximates what Icon Composer
will do.

## Appearances

| Appearance | Background |
|---|---|
| **Default** (also light mode) | `00-background-default-silver.svg`, or Icon Composer's own gradient fill `#ffffff` to `#eef1f8` to `#d6dceb` |
| **Dark** | `00-background-dark-midnight.svg`, or the fill `#24345f` to `#141d3d` to `#0a0f24` |
| **Clear and Tinted** (Mono) | Made by macOS from the layer shapes. See the Mono column below. |

## Layers, bottom to top, with suggested settings

Starting points only, not tried in Icon Composer. Tune by eye in each preview.

| Layer | Liquid Glass | Opacity | Blend | Mono (Clear/Tinted) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 to 06 cards (red to violet) | On | 100% | Normal | Keep. Their stacked edges carry the shape. |
| 07-front-card | **On, high translucency** | about 20 to 30% | Normal | Keep, low opacity. |
| 08-front-sheen | Off | 40 to 60% | Soft Light or Overlay | **Hide.** It turns muddy without colour. |
| 09-prism-light | Off, or light glass | 100% | Normal | 100%. |
| 10-prism-shade | Off | 100% | Normal | **About 55%**, so the prism stays 3D when everything goes white. |

Check light, dark, clear (light and dark) and tinted. Make sure the prism
reads at 16 and 32 px.

## Matches

The menu bar glyph uses the same 3D prism: an off-centre ridge with a light
face and a shaded face. See section 3.4 of `../2.0.0-build-guide.md`.
